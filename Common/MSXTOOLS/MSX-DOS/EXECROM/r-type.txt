R-Type (c) IREM 1988
--------------------

Optimization patch v1.0 by FRS

This patchfile provides several bugfixes and optimizations for
this game.

- New FMPAC detection routine
     The old one was braindamadged and even mishandled the stack,
     FMPAC was only detected if installed on slot1, now any MSX-Music
     is detected on any slot.

- Fixed MSX2 detection routines
     They used a nonstandard method to detect MSX2 by checking the
     amount of VRAM avaiable. Now it uses the standard 002Dh check.

- Optimized HTIMI handler
     Very ligher than the original one.

- Optional invulnerability (only for game-testing  ;)

Warning: There are many bad dumps of the R-type cartridge on the net.
This patch requires a well dumped rom with 3megabits of size (384KB).
