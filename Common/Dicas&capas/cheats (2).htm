For People Who're Too Lazy To Play Properly


 Ah... Cheats! Some love 'em, some hate 'em, but whatever your stance you have to agree that Speccy games excelled at including them. From the cheeky innuendo of Raf Cecco's cheats, to Pete Harrap's ingenious cheat for Auf Wiedersehen Monty they were as varied as they were useful.
Some simply made the game a bit easier, some made the game a total cake-walk. Some - in the case of Zub - opened up a whole sub-game. By Cracky, I love cheats.
Please note that all these cheats have been nicked from old Speccy magazines. Also note that where a cheat tells you to redefine the keys as something to activate the cheat, then you will obviously have to redefine them again before playing.

Arkanoid (Imagine)
Get a high score, then enter your name as PBRAIN, and you'll be able to continue from whatever level you died on by simply pressing the Space Bar from now on.

Arkanoid 2 (Imagine)
Get a high score, then enter your name as MAAAAH, and you'll be able to continue from whatever level you died on by simply pressing the Space Bar from now on.

Army Moves (Dynamic)
The password for the second section of the game is 27351.

Batman - The Movie (Ocean)
Hold down the keys ED209 to advance to the next level.

BMX Freestyle Simulator (Codemasters)
Enter your name as TAEHC and you'll always qualify.

Chase HQ (Ocean)
Define your keys as SHOCKED followed by the Enter key and a test menu will appear. Keys 1 to 5 play the various Chase HQ animations and 6 allows you to enter your name into the high score table. While playing, 1 lets you restart the level, 2 advances you to the next level, Three shows you the completion effect and 4 gives you an extra credit each time you press it.
