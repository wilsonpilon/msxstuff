INSTRUCTIONS

Many years ago, the citizens of the planet Nonterraqueous tried to destroy the evil dictator of their planet with a robotic seeker. This attempt to kill the maniac computer failed.
Now the situation had got much worse. The computer has grown too unstable with age and threatens to self-destruct along with the planet. The people are desperate, and in their desperation have created a robot with the mind of a man.
But the soul of this robot is in torment and only through death can it find peace. However its programming only allows it to die when it has reached the computer's lair. Only then can it explode it's built in bomb.
The game is set in a 16 x 16 maze. This maze is further subdivided into 3 sections. You start off in the first section and must make your way to the third section where the computer is, but to leave a section you must first locate the transporter key and then find the transporting room, which is labelled.
There are obstacles in the way, which you must overcome. There also also floating robotic protectors, which hinder your progress. Watch out for the deadly fungus!

SOLUTION
LEVEL 1

From the start room you'll bounce around a bit before coming to rest in the middle of the room with four phasing platforms. From here head down, grab the Laser and head back up and then carry on right until you can go either up or down. Head down and follow this path around (straight over the phasing platform) until you get to the room with the Force Field Key in it, grab that and head back around to the phasing platform, then down and right, then down, then left, then up and then left over all the phasing platforms here. At the end drop down till you get to the room with the Level Key, so grab that and head back around to the right of the start. Once you're back in the room with the four phasing platforms, head up, left over the phasing platform and then down this side of the level (grabbing the other Force Field Key hidden under a platform along the way) until you reach a room with a phasing platform in it. Jump over this and you're in the Teleport Room of this level. Well done!
LEVEL 2

You start this level in a little spiral segment, so simply follow the level around until you come to a room with a phasing platform, ignore this and carry on and up until you enter a Free Fall room. Use your psyche to get to the top and then head left, and follow these rooms until you get another choice (either up or down). Head down and then left over the phasing platform, and then up this sequence of rooms to get the Level Key at the top. Then head back down, over the phasing platform and right again into the Teleport Room for this level. That's that dealt with, then...
LEVEL 3

This level is very straight forward, simply head up, then left over the first phasing platform and drop through the second one a few rooms later. Then head left, down a few rooms, left over the Deadly Fungus and then down, down and down until you can go no further, then go right and down yet more rooms, though be wary that there's a drop at the bottom of the level. At the bottom, head left then work your way up, and after a few rooms you'll enter an odd room with a sequence of yellow blobs going diagonally from bottom-left to top-right. Either jump or use your psyche to traverse this room and you've completed the game. Phew!

TIPS

The rooms marked as "Free Fall" on the map are devoid of any platforms at all, and should be entered while flying, or you'll plummet to your untimely demise.

The leap strength meter has five settings, though selecting setting four while on the way up the scale will give you a more powerful jump than setting five. Very useful for jumping over deadly fungus!

Don't run out of psyche, it'll kill you stone dead. Equally, don't store too much, that'll fry your brains as well.
