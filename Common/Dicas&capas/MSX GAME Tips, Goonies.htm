Item Tips of The Goonies

 This is a tips for "The Goonies". There are a lot of hidden items in this game. I'll show you. But they may not be all. If you could find the other items, please show me.
 
 
 
 

 If you want to continue your game or keep the item you got before, press "CTRL + K" while it is shown the title screen. After, input the keyword:

The 1st Stage : GOONIES
The 2nd Stage : MR SLOTH
The 3rd Stage : GOON DOCKS
The 4th Stage : DOUBLOON
The 5th Stage : ONE EYED WILLY
