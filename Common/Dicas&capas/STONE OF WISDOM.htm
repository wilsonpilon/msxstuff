Stone of Wisdom

TECHNICS:

1. Kill all enemies, or you won't be able to find the hidden rooms.

2. When your Intel is high, the hidden rooms will appear automatically.

3. Open all Pyramid-cases, but don't get the crystals at once. You may find the hidden rooms if you stab the wall on your sword without you get the crystals.

4. When you arrive the final room and there is not a Stone, the room is false. Try to find the true one.

5. Go to another scene without you get ones, come back soon, and open the Pyramid-cases again if the crystals aren't what you want, and the crystals may be changed.

6. If you want to continue your game, press 'Space', 'Caps', and 'Kana(Code)' in the title screen and input the keywords:
