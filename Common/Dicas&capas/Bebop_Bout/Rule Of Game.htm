Rule of Game

Control Area

    You have four cards every time, and put these on the putting place to attack the opponent.
    You put a card,not in turns, but in real-time.
    The mid putting place is used by both players.

Kinds of Cards

    Punch,Kick
    Attack Points : 1(Blue), 2(Yellow), 3(Red)

      These are cards to attack.
      The color of a card means the attack points.

    Throw
    Attack Points : 3

      This is a card to attack.
      Irrespective of the opponent's defensible points,you can do damage to him.

    Special Attack
    Attack Points : 4

      This is a card to attack.
      You can do much damage to the opponent.

    Defense

      This is a card to increase defensible points.

    Combination
    Attack Points : 4(Blue), 7(Yellow), 10(Red)

      This is a card to attack.
      It's made by yourself.
      The color of a card means the attack points.

Attack

    Any attack cards have a number from 1 to 9. If the number of your card is next to the number of the card on the putting place,you can put it on the place.
    For example, If the number of the card on the putting place is 3,you can put the card numberd 2 or 4. In the case of 1, you can put 9 or 2. In the case of 9, you can put 8 or 1....

Defense

    Defensible points increase by taking a . And its maximum varies from character to character.
    When you are attacked by the opponent, first the defensible points decrease, and then your power points decrease by the odd.
    You should keep defensible points every time for the win.

Do away with a card

    You can do away with your useless card by pushing Button B on the card. And then a new card appears soon.
    The replacement interval varies from character to character.

Combination

    You can make a combination card from three punch or kick crads. The requirements are that they are same color and have consecutive numbers. It's no problem you mix punch with kick.
    For example, a blue punch card numbered 3, a blue kick card numbered 4, and blue punch card numberd 5.
    The number 1 links the number 9. For example, 8-9-1 or 9-1-2, these are right.
    The number of a combination card amount to the number of a card clicked lastly.
    The turn You click cards is free. For example, 3-4-5 or 4-3-5 or 5-4-3...
    How to use a combination card is same as other attack cards.
