Xak: Tower of Gazzel music pack
From www.rpgfan.demon.co.uk

Xak Gazzel on MSX contains MIDI music that will be played back during the introdemo instead
of the usual PSG/FM music. This only works on MSX-Turbo R GT with MIDI output port. This
pack consists of this MIDI music. It is taken straight from the disk (not recorded) so there
loss of data. The files have been converted from RCP to the more usual MID format.

This MIDI music is composed for use with the Roland MT-32. All GS compatible instruments
Have the sounds of MT-32, but in order to use this sounds, you will have to run mt32.mid.
mt32.mid switches your instrument into GS mode, sets reverb, and switches all instruments
to MT-32. You can download mt32.mid from my page.

Hope you like 'em!
