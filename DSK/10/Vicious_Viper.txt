Exploding Atoms
---------------
A strategy game for two players. Place your atoms
on a grid and capture your opponents territory.

Vicious Viper
-------------
Guide the viper around the screen and, as it eats
the fruit, it grows in length - but don't allow
it to touch the edge of the screen or its own
tail or you lose.


TO LOAD EACH PROGRAM PRESS RESET BUTTON AND
   TYPE CLOAD AND PRESS THE RETURN KEY
