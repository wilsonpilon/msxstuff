1) How to Begin

At first, set up MSX-DOS2. There are two ways to start the game.

If you have FS-A1ST(256KB RAM), start batch file: FISH-ST
Or, if you have FS-A1GT(512KB RAM) or FS-A1ST with extend RAM, start batch
file: FISH-GT
Once executed, never pull out the disk out of its drive.

2) How to Play

Only keyboard can be used.

2-a: Preparing Mode

 Soon after execution, you can ses your fishing rod raised. Now is called,
"Preparing Mode." Push left to change devices. Push right to chnage bait.
Push up for going to "Pocketbook Mode."
 After you select the device and bait, push down for starting fishing. Your
reel begins to revolve. While your device is going down, push SPACE to stop
sinking, and go to "Waiting Mode."

 * It stops automatically when depth gets to 8m50cm.

2-b: Waiting Mode

 Now, you are waitnig for fish. Push left for raising your rod to tempt fish.
Push up for interrupt fishing, and your reel is rewound automatically, go back
to "Preparing Mode."
 When fish comes, you'll see the tip of your rod shaking. It means HIT!!.
Let's push SPACE, or fish will steal bait and run away. If you swing up the
rod timely, you can try to draw up fish!!

2-c: Hit Mode

 Now, you can see the fishing boy standing up. Push SPACE to rewind your reel.
Push left to swing up the rod. It'll be easy to draw up fish if you do what we
call POMPING: repeat swing up, and rewind again and again.
 You must be careful when you hear beep sound. It means fish raging under the
sea to run away. Now, don't rewind or swing up against fish's will , or you'll
miss him or her.
 If you succeed to fish, you can see your catch. Push any key for replaying
the game.

2-d: Pockectbook Mode

 In this mode, you can read about devices, bait, and fish. Push up and down to
move coursor. Push SPACE to choice. But those articles are written in Japanese
, sorry....

3) How to Enjoy

 This game have niether GAME OVER nor ENDING. There is no purpose. You have
just to enjoy fishing in the nature with hearing the sound of the wave, seeing
sea gulls flying in the sky.
